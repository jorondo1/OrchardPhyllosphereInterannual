Model 7 (MDLSYC, "The Weatherman") -- formal specification
============================================================

Paste each $$...$$ block below into a Word equation box (Insert >
Equation > switch input to "LaTeX" via the Conversions dropdown, or
Alt+= then type directly if your Word build supports linear LaTeX
input).

This extends Model 6 (model6_MDLSY_latex.txt) with three standardized
control covariates -- everything through the Management x Season
interaction and the Location/Tree/Year/Cultivar effects is identical to
Model 6, repeated here so this file is self-contained.

Naming convention: continues Model 6's plain-letter scheme (a through g),
adding h, j, m for the three new slopes (skipping i, the observation
index, and k, already used below for the residual-cell index).

  a  = Management intercept (May baseline, index-variable form, 2 levels)
  b  = Season slope at Conventional (shared May->July shift)
  c  = Organic's EXTRA Season slope -- the interaction term itself
  d  = Location effect (random, pooled)
  e  = Tree effect (random, pooled)
  f  = Year effect (random, pooled)
  g  = Cultivar effect (fixed, unpooled)
  h  = degree-hours slope (weather control)
  j  = precipitation slope (weather control)
  m  = sequencing-depth slope (detection-effort control)


1. Notation
-----------
For observation i = 1, ..., N (each row is one Tree x Season measurement):

  - H_i          raw Hill_1 diversity index
  - D_i = H_i - 1   shifted diversity, support (0, inf)
  - Mg[i] in {1, 2}     Management: 1 = Conventional, 2 = Organic
  - S_i = Mo[i]-1 in {0, 1}   Season dummy: 0 = May, 1 = July
  - Lo[i] in {1,...,4}  Location (orchard)
  - Tr[i] in {1,...,n_Tr}  Tree (study unit, nested in Location)
  - Yr[i] in {1,2,3}    Year
  - Cv[i] in {1,...,5}  Cultivar
  - X^deg_i     standardized degree-hours, day before sampling
  - X^precip_i  standardized precipitation, 3 days before sampling
  - X^seq_i     standardized log(sequencing depth) -- log first, then
                z-scored (raw depth is right-skewed, ~3.1K-61K reads; see
                MODEL_HISTORY.md for why log matches the detection-effort
                mechanism better than a raw z-score)

k[i], the residual-scale index (unchanged from Model 6):

  k[i] = 2*(Mg[i]-1) + Mo[i]      (Mg,Mo)=(1,1)->1, (1,2)->2, (2,1)->3, (2,2)->4


2. Likelihood
-------------
$$
D_i \sim \mathrm{LogNormal}\!\left(\mu_i,\ \sigma_{k[i]}\right)
$$


3. Linear model for $\mu$
----------------------------
$$
\mu_i = a_{\mathrm{Mg}[i]} + \gamma_{\mathrm{Mg}[i]}\, S_i
+ d_{\mathrm{Lo}[i]} + e_{\mathrm{Tr}[i]} + f_{\mathrm{Yr}[i]} + g_{\mathrm{Cv}[i]}
+ h\,X^{deg}_i + j\,X^{precip}_i + m\,X^{seq}_i
$$

$$
\gamma_{\mathrm{Mg}} = b + c\,(\mathrm{Mg}-1)
$$

Same interaction structure as Model 6 ($b$ = May$\to$July slope at
Conventional, $c$ = Organic's extra slope). $h$, $j$, $m$ are additive
control covariates only, entering neither the interaction nor any random
effect.


4. Random effects
------------------
$$
d_{\mathrm{Lo}} \sim \mathrm{Normal}(0,\ \sigma_d^2), \qquad \mathrm{Lo}=1,\dots,4
$$
$$
e_{\mathrm{Tr}} \sim \mathrm{Normal}(0,\ \sigma_e^2), \qquad \mathrm{Tr}=1,\dots,n_{Tr}
$$
$$
f_{\mathrm{Yr}} \sim \mathrm{Normal}(0,\ \sigma_f^2), \qquad \mathrm{Yr}=1,2,3
$$

(Non-centered reparameterization at sampling time, as in Model 6; not a
separate modeling choice.)


5. Fixed-effect priors
-------------------------
$$
\begin{aligned}
a_{\mathrm{Mg}} &\sim \mathrm{Normal}(2,2), & \mathrm{Mg}&=1,2\\
b &\sim \mathrm{Normal}(0,1)\\
c &\sim \mathrm{Normal}(0,1)\\
g_{\mathrm{Cv}} &\sim \mathrm{Normal}(0,1), & \mathrm{Cv}&=1,\dots,5\\
h &\sim \mathrm{Normal}(0,1)\\
j &\sim \mathrm{Normal}(0,1)\\
m &\sim \mathrm{Normal}(0,1)
\end{aligned}
$$


6. Variance-component priors
-------------------------------
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_{k} &\sim \mathrm{Exponential}(3.46), \qquad k=1,2,3,4\\
\sigma_d &\sim \mathrm{Exponential}(2.31)\\
\sigma_e &\sim \mathrm{Exponential}(2.31)\\
\sigma_f &\sim \mathrm{Exponential}(2.31)
\end{aligned}
$$

Identical rates to Model 6, not a fresh calibration: $h$, $j$, $m$ are
additive $\mu$-level fixed effects (the same category $g_{\mathrm{Cv}}$
already was in Model 6), not new *summed* variance terms, so the number
of variance components feeding $D_i$'s total marginal variance stays at
4 and there's nothing new to rebalance.


7. Derived estimands
-----------------------
Population-level mean at Management $m$, Season $s\in\{0,1\}$, at
reference weather and sequencing depth ($X^{deg}=X^{precip}=X^{seq}=0$,
this sample's own average), floor added back:

$$
\mathrm{E}[H \mid m,s] = 1 + \exp\!\left(a_m + \gamma_m s + \tfrac{1}{2}V_{k(m,s)}\right),
\qquad V_k = \sigma_k^2 + \sigma_d^2 + \sigma_e^2 + \sigma_f^2
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{May gap} &= \mathrm{E}[H\mid 2,0] - \mathrm{E}[H\mid 1,0]\\
\text{July gap} &= \mathrm{E}[H\mid 2,1] - \mathrm{E}[H\mid 1,1]\\
\text{Seasonal change} &= \text{July gap} - \text{May gap}
\end{aligned}
$$

$h$, $j$, $m$ cancel out of every contrast above (same reference value on
both sides of each difference); they only matter for the raw per-cell
means, where they net the estimand of any weather/depth confounding with
Season. Known limitation: $h$, $j$, $m$ are likely correlated with Season
and/or Year in the real data (confirmed for weather in Model 6's own
earlier fit, before it moved here: $X^{deg}$ r=-0.73 with Season). This
widens and correlates their own posteriors; it doesn't bias the joint fit
or any of the three contrasts above, which are built from more than one
coefficient each.
